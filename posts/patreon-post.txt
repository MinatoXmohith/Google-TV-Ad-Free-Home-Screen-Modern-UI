I Took My TV Back: Ad-Free Google TV + a Liquid Glass Dashboard I Designed

My TCL Google TV showed a sponsored ad before I could even open Netflix. So I turned it into a design project: clean up the system, then build a home screen I'd actually want to look at.

WHAT I DID

1. Connected to the TV over Wi-Fi using Android's debug bridge. No root, nothing flashed.
2. Backed up every app and setting first.
3. Switched off 11 junk apps: telemetry, ad feeds and promo apps. Disabled, not deleted, so one command undoes everything.
4. Designed a new home screen in Figma, inspired by Apple's tvOS 2026, and built it as a native Android TV app.

THE NEW DASHBOARD

• A greeting with my name, and a time + profile-photo pill that opens Inputs, Wi-Fi and Settings
• Liquid glass cards: light blur, edge refraction and a bright rim, made to work on a 2021 TV chip with no live blur
• Now playing: shows whatever is on Spotify or YouTube Music, with album art and controls
• Live weather for Nellore
• My favourite apps, plus All apps
• Featured / Movies / Shows: this week's trending posters, grouped by genre

Above: the stock Google TV with its sponsored rows, next to the new dashboard. It went through four versions on the real TV to get here.
