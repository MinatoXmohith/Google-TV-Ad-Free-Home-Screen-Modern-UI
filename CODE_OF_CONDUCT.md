# Code of conduct

Be kind and assume good intent. Help people whose TV or setup is different from yours, and keep
feedback about the work, not the person.

Not OK here: harassment, insults, discrimination, sharing someone's private information, or anything
meant to get into someone else's TV or home network without their permission.

If someone breaks these rules, the maintainer may remove their comments, issues or pull requests, or
block them from the repository. To report something, contact
[@MinatoXmohith](https://github.com/MinatoXmohith) or use GitHub's **Report content** option.
